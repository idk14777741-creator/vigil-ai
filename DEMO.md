# VIGIL AI — 5-minute demo script

Every flow below is seeded and reproducible. Password for all accounts: `Vigil#2024`
Reset everything between runs: **Admin → Overview → ↺ Reset demo data**.

## Act 1 — Priya's steady week (personnel) · ~3 min

Sign in as **priya@vigil.demo** (quick-fill button on the login screen).

1. **Dashboard** — "Good afternoon, Priya." Active shift badge, tasks with an
   overdue highlight, wellness snapshot with the *"Demo · Simulated"* chip,
   and the Recovery Score ring (84) with its factor breakdown.
2. **Shift Monitor** — "You're on shift now" hero with live progress bar.
   The "worth knowing" flags are gentle, never disciplinary.
3. **Tasks** — open the overdue task, drag progress, mark it Done. Toast
   confirms; the supervisor gets pinged (shown later).
4. **Wellness Monitor** — vitals vs *her own* baseline, sleep pattern card,
   "All steady" insight, privacy card stating exactly who can see what.
5. **Recovery Score** — the full transparent formula; "what would help"
   suggestions; 14-day history.
6. **Weekly Report** — week at a glance, mock-AI summary (clearly labelled),
   type a reflection and save it.
7. **VIGIL AI Assistant** — ask *"I'm feeling stressed"* → supportive reply
   with concrete suggestions. Conversations are private, always.
8. **De-stress Zone** — play "Slow Harbour" (generative audio, no licensed
   files), point out the wind-down timer.
9. **Buddy Connect** — Rohan↔Leila connected in the seed; Priya has a fresh
   pending invite to accept live.
10. **Message From Home** — Amma's and Vikram's videos. Play one; the New
    badge fades. Family corner simulates the loved-one upload.

## Act 2 — Rohan's heavy week (the why) · ~1 min

Sign out, sign in as **rohan@vigil.demo**.

- **Dashboard** — Recovery Score **42** ("Time to recharge") after a 16h
  extended-duty night.
- **Shift Monitor** — the *extended duty* flag: "Consider handing over when
  you can, and take a proper break afterwards."
- **Recovery Score** — three kind suggestions with shortcut buttons.
- **Wellness** — "Several short nights" + "Stress running higher" insights.

This is the product's thesis: surface strain early, respond with support,
never with judgement.

## Act 3 — The support network responds · ~1.5 min

Sign in as **medic@vigil.demo** (Dr. Meera Rao):

- **Medic Connection** — queue with "wellness shared" markers (only Rohan
  and Priya authorized it). Open Rohan's thread, reply, set status.
- Emphasize: wellness summaries arrive **only** with the person's explicit
  toggle, revocable any instant.

Sign in as **supervisor@vigil.demo** (Daniel Reiss):

- **Supervisor Connection** — workload queue (Leila's lighter-block request
  open on top). Reply, resolve.
- **Tasks** — assign a new task; the assignee is notified instantly.
- **Incident Reporting** — triage queue sorted by severity; open Rohan's
  near miss, add a note, resolve it with a resolution (required field).
- Emphasize: **no wellness route exists for supervisors at all** — by design.

Sign in as **admin@vigil.demo**:

- **Admin Panel** — overview stats, users, audit log (every sensitive action
  traceable), the Integrations card (live-mode readiness), and ↺ Reset demo
  data for the next run.

## Closing line

> "VIGIL AI doesn't diagnose and doesn't surveil. It gives the person a calm
> picture of their own week, and gives the support network a respectful way
> to respond — with the person in control of every boundary."
